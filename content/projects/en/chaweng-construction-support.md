A regular client building a private residence in Chaweng brought us in as ongoing groundwork support throughout the construction process. We worked alongside the main contractor across multiple stages, typically in half-day and full-day shifts, handling everything that required machinery.

## Site leveling and earthworks

We started by bringing the plot to a working level — cutting high spots, redistributing material and hauling surplus soil off site with the Hino 300. The Caterpillar 305CR handled the bulk earthmoving while the Komatsu PC30-7 worked in tighter areas around the perimeter and between structures.

## Foundation excavation and pile support

Once the slab area was established, we excavated pile positions to the specified depths. We then worked alongside the crane team to position and install foundation piles, pipes and other structural elements — providing the excavation, clearance and ground support that made their work possible.

## Utilities and drainage

We dug the trenches for underground services — water, drainage and sewage lines. The site had waterlogging in places, so we also dewatered low-lying areas, graded the ground for proper runoff and installed the necessary underground infrastructure. The plot was left with a clean, dry working surface and functioning drainage before handover to the next trades.

## What we bring to a construction site

We're not a general contractor, but we cover the groundwork that runs through the full build cycle — excavation, earthmoving, foundation support, utility trenching and drainage. If you're building on Koh Samui and need reliable machinery with experienced operators available across multiple stages, we're set up for exactly that.
