Steel and metalwork deliveries on Koh Samui come with a specific challenge: the pieces are often large, rigid and custom-made, which means any damage in transit means delays and extra costs for the client. This job was delivering a set of semicircular steel arches - fabricated for the entrance door and window openings of a private villa under construction in Chaweng.

## Loading and transport

The arches vary in size, and the largest - the door arch - needed careful positioning on the truck bed to avoid any stress on the curve. We loaded everything onto the Hino 300, distributed the weight properly and secured each piece before moving. The route to the Chaweng site is straightforward, but oversized metalwork still requires a steady hand and attention to securing points at every stop.

## Delivery and unloading

The truck arrived at the building site on schedule. Unloading was done carefully alongside the wall where the arches were going in, keeping the crew's work as short as possible. From pickup to drop-off the job wrapped up in a couple of hours.

## Result

All arches arrived intact, on time and ready to go straight into installation. No bends, no scratches, no waiting around. For a construction site where trades are lined up and timelines are tight, that's what a reliable delivery looks like.
