We've worked with this client for a long time. The brief doesn't change: pick up steel structures from the supplier and get them to the construction site on time, every time. No complications, no delays, no excuses.

The project was a new build in Chong Mon - one of the quieter parts of the island, but the site was active and on a tight schedule. Steel structures needed to arrive when the crews were ready to work with them, not before and definitely not after.

## How the runs work

We use two trucks depending on what's being moved. The Hino Super FM18 - a 10-wheel heavy truck - handles the larger and heavier loads. The Hino Ranger 4D, a 6-wheel dump truck with a crane behind the cab, takes the smaller batches and is useful when unloading needs a lift rather than a forklift. Sometimes both trucks run on the same day, sometimes only one. We match the truck to the load.

At the supplier warehouse, a forklift handles loading. Our drivers know the routine - they arrive, the steel goes on, we secure everything and move. At the site, unloading is coordinated with the construction team so the material lands exactly where it needs to be.

## Why they keep calling us

Punctuality. Construction schedules on Koh Samui are tight, and a late steel delivery holds up every trade waiting on it - welders, builders, the whole crew stands around. Our client books us because when we confirm a delivery, it happens. That track record is why a one-time job turned into a regular contract, and why we're still running these deliveries.

We carry steel, prefabricated elements, construction materials and other heavy or oversized loads to sites across Koh Samui. If you need reliable truck deliveries for your project, get in touch.
