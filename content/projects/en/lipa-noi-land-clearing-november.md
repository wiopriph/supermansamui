A plot in Lipa Noi, on the west coast of Koh Samui, had been abandoned for several years. The villas next door are already built and fenced, while this land had disappeared under waist-high grass and bushes - you could not walk across it, let alone start building. The client asked us to clear the land down to bare soil and level it for the next stage of construction.

## What we found under the overgrowth

As we stripped the vegetation, an old fallen tree turned up underneath it. The trunk was lying in the grass and the root system had gone deep into the ground. Finds like this are common on abandoned plots in Samui: the grass hides stumps, trunks and roots that have been rotting in the soil for years.

They cannot be left there. Organic matter in the ground keeps decomposing, the soil settles, and voids open up under a foundation or driveway. So we uprooted the tree completely: pulled out the trunk, dug out the roots and removed all the organic debris from the site.

## Equipment on site

The main work was done by the Yanmar ViO70, a 7-tonne zero tail swing excavator. It cut the bushes, pulled out the trunk, dug out the roots and piled the debris for loading. The zero tail swing design let it work right up against the neighbouring villa's concrete wall without touching the fence while slewing.

For the final grading we brought in a Kubota tractor with a front blade: it spread the soil across the plot and smoothed out the track marks. Hauling was done by the Hino 300 dump truck: the first trip took the trunks and large branches, and two more trips carried the green waste and smaller organic debris.

## How the day went

We arrived in the morning and the plot was clean by the evening. The sequence is our standard one for land clearing:

1. Cut the grass and bushes across the whole area and gathered them into piles.
2. Uprooted the old tree, pulled out the trunk and roots.
3. Hauled the trees and organic waste off site - three truck trips.
4. Leveled the surface so no holes were left where the roots had been.

## Result

One day of excavator work, a tractor for grading, three truck trips. An overgrown plot turned into a flat site with clean soil: no roots, no stumps, no organic matter. The client got land that surveyors and builders can walk onto straight away.
